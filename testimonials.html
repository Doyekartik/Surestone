ABOUT US

The Astittva Estates Properties.

We help families and businesses buy, sell and lease property in Atal Nagar (Naya Raipur), Raipur and Bangalore. We work with checked projects, clear prices and one person who stays with you from the first visit to the day you get the keys.

Who we are

The Astittva Estates is a real estate group from Raipur. Our team knows the city area by area: which projects are moving fast, which builders deliver on time, and what a fair price looks like. We also work in Bangalore, so families moving between the two cities can deal with one team they already know.

We are not a listing portal. Every home on this website is a project our team has seen, checked and is ready to show you in person.

What we do

  • Buying a homeFlats, villas, penthouses and plots: ready to move or under construction.
  • New projectsNew launches from trusted developers, often with launch prices and better choice of units.
  • Selling your propertyA fair price, the right buyers and help with every paper till registration.
  • Leasing and commercialShops, offices and retail space to buy or to rent.
  • Home loans and paperworkHelp with bank loans, agreements, registration and possession.
  • After you move inInteriors, resale or renting it out later: we are still a call away.
